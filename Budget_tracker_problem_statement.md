Personal Budget and Savings Goal Tracker


Problem Statement:
Nowadays, students in particular, under peer pressure, try to live up to the expectations of their friends, forgetting that they have futures to build. Most students receive allowance from their parents and sponsors yet, they just play around with their money, not knowing that tomorrow can change. They do not know that their allowances can cease to be given due to unforeseen circumstances in the future. So, instead of leaving up to expectations, why not build a foundation that if your allowance stops, you will survive?

GCGO Linkage. 
One of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UNSDGs) is to eradicate extreme poverty and hunger. This goal directly links to the GCGOs, especially Job Creation and economic growth. If students can be financially responsible, they can create jobs for themselves. But how can this financial responsibility come about? If students and young adults are able to track their spendings, they can notice aspects in which they mismanaged their funds and adjust it. They can set goals within particular periods of time and try to attain their goals. So, my team and I created a personal budget and saving goals tracker to enable users to be financially responsible. 

Target users and objectives
The primary users of this application are university students, young professionals, users working towards a specific savings goal, and others who want to improve on financial responsibility. The main objective of the application is to record income and expenses, track progress towards savings goals, allow users to search and manage their transactions, keep financial records permanently, and provide summaries of spendings and income. 

Functional Requirements
The system allows users to manage their transactions by providing room for adding new transactions, viewing all their transactions, updating transactions and deleting transactions. It also provides an interface for searching and filtering transactions. A user could search a transaction by category and also filter their transactions. The application also provides functions such as calculating expenses, checking spendings by category, and displaying saving progress as a percentage. In a case where a user happens to leave the application half way whether intentionally or not, they have nothing to fear because the application will save their files and they can continue from where they stopped. 

Data fields
The application has fields such as user ID for unique identification, category to know what the money was spent on, amount to know the amount spent, description for the details of the spending and date for the date of the transaction. It also has saving fields such as goal to know the goal of the savings, target amount to know the amount of money you will be spending, saved amount for easy progress tracking and a deadline for the savings. 

The application is easy to handle because it has a menu-driven interface for easy navigation, handles invalid user inputs and provides friendly feedback for invalid inputs, stores data reliably, uses clear prompts and is organized into separate python modules. 

This project will be considered successful if users can create, read, update and delete their records. IT will also be considered successful if users’ data remain available after a restart. If the application can run without errors, does not store invalid data, and all functions operate very well, then the program is successful.
